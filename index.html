01INTERNAL PRODUCT · INDIA + US

RSAR Smart Stack

What happens when the easiest thing to build isn't the hardest problem to solve?

The Smart Stack became a team productivity platform across India and the US. The interesting part wasn't the repository. It was deciding what belonged in it, removing a dependency that slowed learning, and making adoption part of the product.

01 · WHAT WAS BROKEN?

Useful Python utilities and productivity ideas existed, but there was no central, easy-to-adopt platform for the team. The first version also mixed utilities with AI use cases, creating production dependencies before the core product had been validated.

02 · WHAT DID I DECIDE — AND NOT DO?

I removed AI from the initial scope. The goal was to experiment fast, learn from usage, and avoid letting production AI tollgates become the bottleneck. I also introduced an intake lens around usage, time spent, potential time saved and people impacted before adding tools.

03 · WHAT SHIPPED?

A Python-only central utility platform, a contributor-friendly structure with README guidance and standards, automated environment setup, monthly release notes and live demos to drive awareness and adoption.

04 · WHAT CHANGED?

The project evolved from “a repository of tools” into a repeatable team productivity platform. Adoption, contribution and feedback became part of the product rather than activities that happened after launch.

WHAT I LEARNED

The hardest product problem was not building the central repository. It was deciding what deserved to exist inside it and creating a loop that made people return, contribute and improve it.

03CONSUMER PRODUCT · CONCEPT

PharmEasy

Improving retention through automated medication management.

01 · WHAT WAS BROKEN?

Chronic patients can struggle with recurring medication management, creating friction around remembering doses and re-ordering medication after the first purchase.

02 · WHAT DID I DECIDE — AND NOT DO?

I evaluated multiple directions and prioritized an integrated Reminder & Stock Management System over a social “Family Track” dashboard.

03 · WHAT WOULD SHIP?

A Reminder Tab that deducts medication stock based on daily dosage and alerts users when fewer than 10 days remain.

04 · WHAT WOULD CHANGE?

If shipped, I would measure notification click-through and churn after the “Reminder to Buy” alert.
